# Transformer loading and oil temperature

Insulation ageing in oil-immersed power transformers accelerates with hot-spot temperature, so sustained overload shortens life even without an immediate fault. Top-oil temperature normally tracks load with a thermal lag of several hours. Oil temperature that rises without a matching load increase can indicate reduced cooling (fans, pumps, blocked radiators) or internal heating.

Dissolved-gas analysis, partial-discharge monitoring and winding-resistance tests complement online temperature trending. Alarm and trip limits must be taken from the nameplate and the utility's own standards; this note does not specify any.
