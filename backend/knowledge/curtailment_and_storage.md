# Renewable curtailment and battery storage

Curtailment is the deliberate reduction of renewable output that could otherwise be produced. Common causes are transmission limits between a resource area and load centres, system minimum-load or oversupply conditions, and stability constraints.

Battery energy storage can absorb energy that would be curtailed and release it during the evening peak, which reduces the residual load served by flexible hydro and thermal plant. Key sizing parameters are power (MW), energy (MWh), round-trip efficiency, a state-of-charge reserve, and degradation per unit of throughput. Siting storage on the constrained side of a bottleneck is typically more effective than siting it at the load centre.
