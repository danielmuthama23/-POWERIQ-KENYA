# Technical and non-technical electricity losses

Technical losses are physical: conductor resistance (I squared R), transformer core and copper losses, and corona on high-voltage lines. They grow roughly with the square of current, so loading and conductor length matter.

Non-technical (commercial) losses are energy that is delivered but not billed: illegal connections and meter tampering, faulty or unread meters, billing errors and unmetered supply. They are estimated as unaccounted energy minus the technical-loss estimate.

A standard screening approach compares energy input at a feeder or transformer with the sum of customer meter readings, subtracts a technical-loss estimate, and ranks feeders by the residual. Flagged feeders should be confirmed through field inspection and meter audits before any enforcement action.
