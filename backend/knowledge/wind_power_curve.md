# Wind turbine power curve and forecast error

Turbine output is zero below cut-in speed, rises roughly with the cube of wind speed up to rated speed, stays at rated power until cut-out, then shuts down. Because of the cubic region, small wind-speed errors cause large power errors, and forecast skill degrades with lead time. Persistence-type baselines are therefore hard to beat at very short horizons, and weather-driven models gain value at longer ones.
