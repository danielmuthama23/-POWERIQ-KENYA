# Hydropower and rainfall variability

Reservoir hydropower output depends on stored water, head and turbine availability. Storage smooths short-term rainfall variation but prolonged dry periods reduce available energy and shift load onto thermal plant. POWERIQ summarises this as a 'water factor' (0.45 to 0.95 in the synthetic data) derived from smoothed rainfall; it is a modelling convenience, not a measured reservoir level.

East African rainfall has two main seasons (long rains and short rains) with strong year-to-year variability, so hydro availability planning should consider seasonal forecasts alongside inflow measurements.
